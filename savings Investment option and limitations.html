1 · Post Office Savings Account (SB)

Post Office Savings Account Scheme, 2019
4.00%per annum
Tenor No maturity — open-ended. No lock-in of any kind. Extension Not applicable. Amounts Min opening / min balance ₹500. Min withdrawal ₹50. No maximum. Where to open Post offices only. Cheque book, ATM, internet and mobile banking available on request. Interest & payout 4% p.a. on the minimum balance between the 10th and last day of each month. Credited once at the end of each financial year. No interest for any month where that minimum balance falls below ₹500. Max accounts One single account per individual. The same person may additionally be a joint holder in another account. Who can open Single adult · joint (max 3 adults) · minor aged 10+ in own name · guardian for a minor or person of unsound mind. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF · trusts, societies, institutions. Joint account Yes — Joint A (both/all to operate together) or Joint B (either or survivor). Conversion single ⇄ joint is permitted. Transfer Transferable between post offices. Cannot be transferred to another person. Premature rules No penalty. Free withdrawal subject to ₹500 min balance. Inactive for 3 consecutive financial years → silent account; revive with fresh KYC. Balance below ₹500 at FY-end → ₹50 maintenance fee; account closed if balance becomes nil. Tax treatment No 80C Interest exempt under Sec 10(15)(i) up to ₹3,500 (single account) / ₹7,000 (joint account). Beyond those amounts, 80TTA (₹10,000 for non-seniors) or 80TTB (₹50,000 for seniors 60+) applies — old regime only.

⚠ Exam trap — Two separate exemptions apply to SB interest: Sec 10(15)(i) first (₹3,500/₹7,000), then 80TTA/80TTB on whatever remains. These ₹3,500/₹7,000 limits are unique to the SB account and do not apply to any other scheme.

2 · National Savings Recurring Deposit (RD)

National Savings Recurring Deposit Account Rules, 2019
6.70%qtrly comp.
Tenor 5 years = 60 monthly instalments. Extension Yes — a further 5 years on application (Form-3), at the rate prevailing on the extension date. Repeatable. Alternatively may be retained without fresh deposits for up to 5 years, earning the SB rate (4%). Amounts Min ₹100 per month, multiples of ₹10. No maximum. Where to open Post offices. Online via DOP internet banking / IPPB app. Interest & payout 6.70% p.a. compounded quarterly. Entire principal + interest paid as a lump sum at maturity only. Max accounts Any number of accounts, individually or jointly. Who can open Single adult · joint (max 3) · minor 10+ · guardian for minor / person of unsound mind. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF · companies and trusts. Joint account Yes, up to 3 adults. Conversion single ⇄ joint permitted. Can be pledged as security. Default & rebate
  • Default fee: ₹1 per ₹100 of instalment per month of delay.
  • More than 4 defaults → account discontinued; revivable within 2 months of the 4th default on payment of arrears + fees.
  • Advance rebate: 6 advance deposits → ₹10 per ₹100 denomination rebate; 12 advance deposits → ₹40 per ₹100.
 Loan Up to 50% of the balance, available after 12 deposits completed (one full year). Interest: 2% p.a. above the RD rate. Repayable in lump sum or instalments; unrepaid loan + interest recovered from maturity value. Premature closure Allowed only after 3 years from the date of opening. Interest is then paid at the PO Savings Account rate (4%) — irrespective of how long the RD ran. Not allowed at all in the first 3 years (except on death). Tax treatment No 80C · interest fully taxable TDS under Sec 194A if interest exceeds the threshold.

⚠ Exam trap — RD premature closure gives the SB rate (4%), not "rate minus 2%". TD uses "rate minus 2%" for early exit. These are the two most commonly swapped penalties. Know: RD → SB rate; TD → TD rate less 2%.

3 · National Savings Time Deposit / Post Office TD (POTD)

National Savings Time Deposit Account Rules, 2019
6.9–7.5%by tenure
Tenor & rates Four buckets: 1 yr (6.90%) · 2 yr (7.00%) · 3 yr (7.10%) · 5 yr (7.50%). Extension Yes, for an identical term. Window from maturity date to apply: 6 months (1-yr), 12 months (2-yr), 18 months (3-yr and 5-yr). Auto-renewal can be opted at opening. Amounts Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹100. No maximum. Where to open Post offices. Can be pledged to banks and financial institutions as security. Interest & payout Calculated quarterly but credited / paid annually. Annual interest credited to the holder's linked SB account. Interest not withdrawn earns no further return. Max accounts Any number in any tenure bucket, individually or jointly. Who can open Single adult · joint (max 3) · minor 10+ · guardian. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF. Joint account Yes, up to 3 adults. Conversion single ⇄ joint permitted. Premature closure
  • Before 6 months — not permitted at all.
  • 6 to 12 months — only the SB rate (4%) paid.
  • After 1 year (1/2/3-yr TDs) — completed years earn TD rate less 2%; broken period beyond that earns SB rate.
  • 5-yr TD closed after 4 years — earns the SB rate (4%) for the entire period. (Nov 2023 amendment; previously the 3-yr TD rate applied.)
 Tax treatment 5-yr TD only: Sec 80C 1/2/3-yr TDs carry no 80C deduction. Interest is fully taxable in all four buckets. TDS under Sec 194A applies if interest exceeds the threshold.

⚠ Exam trap — Only the 5-year TD qualifies for 80C — and even then, the interest earned is taxable each year. It is an ETE instrument, not EEE. Premature closure before 5 years defeats the 80C deduction claimed. Also note: the 5-yr TD closed after year 4 now earns the SB rate (4%), not the 3-yr TD rate.

4 · Post Office Monthly Income Scheme (MIS / POMIS)

National Savings Monthly Income Account Rules, 2019
7.40%paid monthly
Tenor 5 years. Extension No extension facility. Close and reinvest into a new account on maturity. If left unclosed, balance earns the SB rate for up to 2 years only. Amounts
  • Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹1,000.
  • Max ₹9 lakh (single account).
  • Max ₹15 lakh (joint account) — raised from ₹9L in Budget 2023.
  • Guardian on behalf of minor: separate limit of ₹3 lakh.
 Where to open Post offices. Interest & payout 7.4% p.a. paid as equal monthly instalments from one month after opening. Credited to the linked SB account or via ECS. Unclaimed monthly interest earns no further interest. Max accounts Any number of accounts, but the aggregate across all accounts must stay within the ₹9L (single) / ₹15L (joint) ceiling. In a joint account, each holder's share is counted equally against his individual ₹9L headroom. Who can open Single adult · joint (max 3 adults) · minor 10+ · guardian for a minor. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF. Joint account Yes, up to 3 adults; ₹15L ceiling for joint. Conversion single ⇄ joint permitted. Transfer Between post offices. Can be pledged as security. Premature closure
  • Before 1 year — not permitted.
  • After 1 year, before 3 years — 2% of principal deducted.
  • After 3 years, before 5 years — 1% of principal deducted.
  • Interest already paid out is not recovered. On death: no deduction; nominee receives principal plus interest up to the preceding month.
 Tax treatment No 80C · interest fully taxable No TDS at the post office on MIS interest, but the holder must declare and pay tax on it.

⚠ Exam trap — MIS penalty is a deduction from principal (2% / 1%), not a cut in the interest rate. Compare SCSS: same style of penalty. TD and RD instead cut the interest rate. Know the two groups: (MIS, SCSS) → principal deduction; (TD, RD) → rate reduction.

5 · Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme (SCSS)

Senior Citizens' Savings Scheme Rules, 2019 (amended Nov 2023)
8.20%paid quarterly
Tenor 5 years. Extension Yes — unlimited successive 3-year blocks (Nov 2023 amendment removed the earlier "once only" restriction). Apply within 1 year of maturity. Extended block earns the rate prevailing on the date of maturity of the original / previous block. Amounts Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹1,000. Max ₹30 lakh aggregate across all SCSS accounts (raised from ₹15L in Budget 2023). Where to open Post offices and authorised banks — all public-sector banks plus ICICI, HDFC, Axis and IDBI Bank. Interest & payout 8.2% p.a. simple interest, paid quarterly on 1 April, 1 July, 1 October and 1 January. Not compounded. Unclaimed quarterly interest earns no further return. Max accounts Multiple accounts permitted, subject to the ₹30L aggregate ceiling. Who can open
  • Any individual aged 60 or above.
  • Aged 55–60 — retired on superannuation or under VRS / special VRS.
  • Aged 50+ retired defence personnel — civilian defence employees are excluded.
  • For the 55–60 and 50+ categories: account must be opened within 3 months of receiving retirement benefits (relaxed from 1 month by the Nov 2023 amendment) and the deposit cannot exceed the retirement benefit amount received.
 Who cannot open NRIs · PIOs · HUF. Joint account Only with the spouse. The entire deposit is attributed to the first / primary holder for the ₹30L ceiling and for tax purposes. The spouse need not meet the age requirement. Transfer Between post offices and banks. Transfer fee: ₹5 per ₹1,000 of deposit. No loan facility and cannot be pledged. Premature closure
  • Before 1 year — all interest paid out is recovered; only principal returned.
  • 1 to 2 years — 1.5% of deposit amount deducted.
  • 2 to 5 years — 1% of deposit amount deducted.
  • During an extended block — no deduction if closed after 1 year into that extension block.
  • On death — no penalty; interest at SCSS rate up to date of death, SB rate thereafter. A surviving spouse who is joint holder may continue the account in his/her own name.
 Tax treatment 80C on deposit Interest is fully taxable. TDS under Sec 194A where interest exceeds ₹1,00,000 p.a. for a senior citizen (raised from ₹50,000 in Budget 2025). Sec 80TTB deduction of up to ₹50,000 on interest income available to seniors under the old regime.

⚠ Exam trap — Three tested facts: (a) 55–60 and 50+ defence entries are conditional on the source of money (retirement benefits) AND a 3-month window; (b) joint holding is restricted to the spouse only — and the ceiling attaches to the first holder alone; (c) the SCSS extension cap was "once only" before Nov 2023 — old books are wrong.

6 · National Savings Certificate, VIII Issue (NSC)

National Savings Certificates (VIIIth Issue) Rules, 2019
7.70%annual comp.
Tenor 5 years. ₹1,000 grows to approximately ₹1,449 at maturity. Extension None. On maturity, invest proceeds into a fresh NSC if desired. Amounts Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹100. No maximum — though the 80C deduction is capped at ₹1.5L across all eligible instruments. Where to open Post offices only. Interest & payout 7.7% compounded annually. Paid entirely at maturity — purely cumulative; no interim cash flows. Max accounts Any number of certificates. Who can open Single adult · joint up to 3 adults (Type A — payable jointly; Type B — payable to either or survivor) · minor 10+ · guardian. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF · trusts, companies, societies. Joint account Yes — Joint A and Joint B variants, up to 3 adults. Transfer Freely transferable between post offices. Transfer to another person is permitted only in specific cases: on the holder's death to nominee/legal heir; on a court order; to the pledgee (Gazetted Officer); or from a deceased joint holder to the surviving joint holder. Can be pledged as security to banks, government bodies and housing finance companies. Premature closure Not permitted except: (a) death of the holder or any holder in a joint account; (b) forfeiture by a pledgee who is a Gazetted Officer; (c) court order. Effectively a hard 5-year lock-in in all other cases. Tax treatment 80C on purchase
  • Interest accrues annually and must be declared as "Income from Other Sources" each year — even though no cash is received.
  • Years 1–4: the accrued interest is deemed reinvested in NSC and therefore qualifies as a fresh 80C deduction in that year (within the ₹1.5L limit), effectively cancelling the tax for most investors.
  • Year 5: the final year's interest is paid out at maturity, taxable as income, and has no corresponding 80C offset because money leaves the scheme.
  • No TDS is deducted at source.

⚠ Exam trap — The deemed-reinvestment rule is the single most tested NSC concept. Years 1–4 interest: taxable as income, but offset by 80C deduction → effectively neutral for those with unused 80C room. Year 5 interest: taxable income with no 80C offset. Also remember: NSC can be pledged (unlike PPF); PPF cannot.

7 · Kisan Vikas Patra (KVP)

Kisan Vikas Patra Rules, 2019
7.50%115 months
Tenor 115 months (9 years 7 months) at the current 7.5% rate. The tenor is not fixed — it moves inversely with the rate, because the scheme's design promise is that money doubles at maturity. Extension None. Reinvest proceeds in a fresh KVP if desired. Amounts Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹100. No maximum. Where to open Post offices and designated public-sector banks. Interest & payout 7.5% compounded annually. Entire corpus (original amount × 2) paid at maturity. Nothing in between. Max accounts Any number of certificates. Who can open Single adult · joint up to 3 adults (Type A / Type B) · minor 10+ · guardian. Who cannot open NRIs · HUF · trusts and companies. Joint account Yes — Joint A and Joint B, up to 3 adults. Transfer Between post offices freely. Transfer to another person only in the same specific circumstances as NSC. Can be pledged as security. Premature closure Permitted after 2 years 6 months (30 months) from the date of deposit, at the value prescribed in the applicable tables. Also allowed before 30 months on death of holder, forfeiture by a Gazetted-Officer pledgee, or court order. Tax treatment No 80C · interest fully taxable Interest is taxable on accrual or receipt, as consistently followed. No TDS.

⚠ Exam trap — KVP looks like NSC but gets zero tax benefit. Its selling points are capital doubling and liquidity from month 30. The exam pair to drill: NSC = 80C deduction; KVP = no tax benefit. Also: KVP tenor changes each quarter when the rate changes; NSC tenor is fixed at 5 years.

8 · Public Provident Fund (PPF)

Public Provident Fund Scheme, 2019
7.10%annual comp.
Tenor 15 financial years, counted from the end of the FY in which the account was opened — so an account opened in any month of FY 2024-25 matures on 31 March 2040. Extension In blocks of 5 years, unlimited times, in either mode:
  • With fresh contributions — apply within 1 year of maturity (Form 4). Up to 60% of the balance at the start of the block may be withdrawn over the block (once per FY).
  • Without contributions — the default if no application is filed. Balance keeps earning the PPF rate; any amount may be withdrawn once per FY. Once in this mode, contributions cannot resume.
 Amounts Min ₹500 per FY. Max ₹1,50,000 per FY, multiples of ₹50. The ₹1.5L cap is combined across the holder's own account plus any minor account he operates as guardian. Any number of contributions per year (the old 12-deposit limit was removed in 2019). Where to open Post offices and authorised banks (all PSBs and major private banks including SBI, HDFC, ICICI, Axis, Kotak). Interest & payout Compounded annually. Interest computed on the lowest balance between the close of the 5th day and the last day of each month. Credited on 31 March each year. Deposit on or before the 5th to earn interest for that month. Entire corpus paid at maturity. Max accounts Strictly one account per individual. A guardian may additionally hold one per minor ward. A second own account is irregular: merged with the first, earns zero interest for the excess, and may attract regulatory action. Who can open Any resident individual in his/her own name; a guardian on behalf of a minor or person of unsound mind. Who cannot open
  • NRIs cannot open a new account.
  • HUF cannot open (barred since 2005).
  • A resident who becomes NRI — existing account continues to maturity at the PPF rate but cannot be extended. From 1 Oct 2024, accounts where NRI status was undisclosed earn no interest retrospectively.
 Joint account Not permitted under any circumstances. Nomination up to one or more persons is allowed (nomination changes are now free). Transfer Freely transferable between post offices and banks with no loss of continuity. Not transferable to another person. Cannot be pledged as security. Loan facility Available from the start of the 3rd FY to the end of the 6th FY from account opening.
  • Maximum: 25% of the balance at the end of the 2nd preceding FY (not the current balance).
  • Interest: 1% p.a. above the prevailing PPF rate (currently 8.1% p.a.) if repaid within 36 months.
  • If not repaid within 36 months: interest jumps to 6% p.a. flat (charged from the date of loan disbursal retroactively).
  • Repayment: principal first, then interest (max 2 monthly instalments for interest portion).
  • Second loan only after first is fully cleared.
 Partial withdrawal From the 7th FY onwards. Once per financial year. Maximum: 50% of the lower of (a) balance at end of the 4th FY immediately preceding the year of withdrawal, or (b) balance at end of the immediately preceding FY. Premature closure Only after 5 complete financial years, and only for three reasons:
  • Life-threatening illness of the holder, spouse, dependent children or parents (with medical certificate).
  • Higher education of the holder or dependent children (with admission proof).
  • Change of residency status to NRI (with emigration documents).
Penalty: interest recomputed at 1% below the rate credited, from the date of opening or from the start of the current 5-year extension block — whichever is relevant. Tax treatment 80C on depositEEE status Contributions deductible under 80C. Interest exempt under Sec 10(11). Maturity proceeds fully exempt. Balance is immune from court attachment for the holder's debts — a unique statutory protection not available in any other small savings scheme.

⚠ Exam trap — Four separate year-based rules for PPF: deposit before the 5th of each month (interest); loan from the 3rd to 6th FY; partial withdrawal from the 7th FY; premature closure after 5 complete FYs. The 15-year clock starts from the end of the FY of opening, not the calendar date. And partial withdrawal is capped on the lower of two balances — the earlier, smaller balance almost always binds.

9 · Sukanya Samriddhi Account (SSY)

Sukanya Samriddhi Account Scheme, 2019
8.20%annual comp.
Tenor Account matures 21 years from the date of opening — or on the girl's marriage after she turns 18, whichever is earlier. Deposits are required only for the first 15 years; balance earns interest for the remaining 6 years with no further deposits needed. Extension No extension. After 21 years the account earns no interest and the corpus should be withdrawn. Amounts Min ₹250 per FY. Max ₹1,50,000 per FY, multiples of ₹50. Aggregated across all accounts opened for the same girl. Where to open Post offices and authorised banks. Interest & payout 8.2% compounded annually; credited at the end of each FY. Entire corpus paid as lump sum on maturity. Max accounts One account per girl child. Maximum two accounts per family. A third is allowed only where twins/triplets are born as the second birth, or triplets at the first birth — with a birth certificate and affidavit. Who can open A natural or legal guardian, for a girl child below 10 years of age at opening. The girl herself operates the account once she turns 18. Who cannot open Girl aged 10 or above at opening · NRI girl child. From Oct 2024: accounts opened by grandparents who are not legal guardians must be transferred to the natural/legal guardian. Joint account Not permitted. Single girl-child account only. Nomination allowed. Transfer Free on production of proof of the guardian/girl relocating; otherwise a transfer fee applies. Transferable between post offices and banks anywhere in India. Default Failure to deposit the ₹250 minimum makes the account irregular. Revive by paying ₹50 penalty per defaulted year + arrears, within 15 years of opening. Partial withdrawal After the girl attains 18 or passes the 10th standard (whichever is earlier): up to 50% of balance at the end of the preceding FY, for higher education. Available as a lump sum or up to 5 annual instalments. Premature closure
  • Marriage after age 18 — permitted from 1 month before to 3 months after the date of marriage (documentary proof required).
  • Death of the girl child — balance paid to guardian with interest.
  • After 5 years, on extreme compassionate grounds — life-threatening illness of the holder or death of the guardian.
  • Any other ground of hardship — closure permitted but interest paid only at the SB rate (4%).
 Tax treatment 80C on depositEEE status Deposits deductible under 80C. Interest exempt under Sec 10(11A). Maturity proceeds fully exempt.

⚠ Exam trap — Three numbers that confuse: girl must be below 10 at opening; deposits required only for 15 years; account matures at 21 years. Partial withdrawal trigger is 18 years or 10th standard pass, whichever is earlier — the 10th standard clause is often missed. SSY and PPF each have their own ₹1.5L/FY ceiling — they do not share one.

10 · Mahila Samman Savings Certificate (MSSC) — Subscription closed

Mahila Samman Savings Certificate, 2023 — window closed 31 March 2025; existing certificates run to maturity
7.50%closed
Status No new accounts from 1 Apr 2025 A one-time scheme introduced in Budget 2023 for a two-year window (1 Apr 2023 – 31 Mar 2025). Not extended in Budget 2025. Existing certificates continue to their 2-year maturity. Still examined in theory papers. Tenor 2 years from the date of deposit. No extension. Amounts Min ₹1,000, multiples of ₹100. Max ₹2 lakh per woman (aggregate). A minimum gap of 3 months was required between opening two separate accounts. Where opened Post offices, all public-sector banks, and select private banks (ICICI, HDFC, Axis, etc.). Interest & payout 7.5% p.a. compounded quarterly, paid as a lump sum at maturity. (The only small savings scheme with quarterly compounding and a 2-year tenor.) Who could open Any woman in her own name, or a guardian on behalf of a minor girl. No upper age limit. Men could not open one, even as guardian for a female relative. Who could not open Men · NRIs. Joint account Not permitted. Partial withdrawal Up to 40% of the balance, once, after completing 1 year from opening. Premature closure
  • After 6 months without giving a reason — permitted at a reduced rate of 5.5% p.a.
  • Death of the account holder — full balance with interest up to the preceding month.
  • Life-threatening illness of the holder, or death of the guardian.
  • Before 6 months — not permitted except on death or compassionate grounds.
 Tax treatment No 80C · interest fully taxable

⚠ Exam trap — MSSC has the unique combination: quarterly compounding + 2-year tenor + ₹2L cap + gender restriction. The partial withdrawal limit is 40% (not 50% as in PPF/SSY). Premature exit rate is specifically 5.5% — the only scheme with a named fallback rate lower than its main rate but specified numerically.

11 · National Pension System (NPS)

PFRDA Act, 2013 — regulated by PFRDA, not under the small savings Rules framework
MarketNAV-linked
Structure Tier I — mandatory pension account, locked in. Tier II — voluntary open savings account; available only to existing Tier I holders. Tenor Tier I: from entry until age 60, extendable (in annual increments) up to age 75. Tier II: no tenor and no lock-in — freely withdrawable. Amounts
  • Tier I: min ₹500 per contribution, min ₹1,000 per FY to keep active. No maximum.
  • Tier II: ₹1,000 to open, ₹250 per subsequent contribution, no annual minimum. No maximum.
 Where to open Points of Presence (PoPs) — banks, post offices, brokers — or online via eNPS. Records held by a CRA (Protean / KFintech / CAMS). A single PRAN (Permanent Retirement Account Number) is portable across all employers and locations. Who can open Any Indian citizen, resident or NRI or OCI, aged 18 to 70 at entry. (NPS is the only scheme here where NRIs and OCIs are permitted.) Who cannot open HUF · PIO cardholders who are not OCIs · undischarged insolvents · persons of unsound mind. Joint account Not permitted. NPS is strictly individual. Nomination of up to 3 persons. Investment choice
  • Active choice: subscriber allocates across E (equity, cap 75%), C (corporate bonds), G (gilts/G-Secs), A (alternatives, cap 5%).
  • Auto choice / lifecycle funds: LC75 (aggressive), LC50 (moderate), LC25 (conservative) — equity automatically tapers as the subscriber ages.
  • Fund manager and scheme can be switched up to twice per FY.
 Partial withdrawal After 3 years of account opening. Up to 25% of the subscriber's own contributions (employer contributions and gains excluded). Maximum 3 times in the entire tenure. Permitted reasons: children's higher education/marriage, residential property purchase/construction, treatment of specified critical illnesses, disability, skill development, or starting a business. Normal exit at 60 Minimum 40% of corpus must purchase an annuity; up to 60% taken as tax-free lump sum. If the total corpus is ₹5 lakh or less, the entire amount may be withdrawn in one go. Premature exit Permitted after 5 years of participation. Minimum 80% must be annuitised; only 20% as lump sum. If the total corpus is ₹2.5 lakh or less, full withdrawal is allowed. On death Entire corpus paid to the nominee or legal heir. The annuity requirement is waived — the nominee may take 100% as lump sum. Tax treatment
  • 80CCD(1) — own contribution, within the overall ₹1.5L 80C ceiling; capped at 10% of salary (employees) or 20% of gross total income (self-employed). Old regime.
  • 80CCD(1B) — additional ₹50,000, completely over and above the ₹1.5L limit. Old regime only. Also available for NPS Vatsalya contributions by the parent (Budget 2025).
  • 80CCD(2) — employer's contribution: 14% of basic+DA for central/state government employees; 14% for private-sector employees from FY 2024-25 under the new tax regime; 10% under the old regime for private employers. This is the only NPS benefit available under the new regime.
  • Lump sum on exit (up to 60%) exempt under Sec 10(12A). Annuity income fully taxable in the year of receipt. Net result: EEE on lump sum, EET on annuity.
 NPS Vatsalya Launched September 2024. For minors below 18, opened and operated by the guardian. Minimum ₹1,000 per year. Converts automatically into a regular Tier I account when the child turns 18. Parent can claim the 80CCD(1B) additional ₹50,000 deduction on Vatsalya contributions (Budget 2025 provision).

⚠ Exam trap — (a) 80CCD(1B) is the only way to get a deduction beyond ₹1.5L on own contribution; (b) 80CCD(2) is the only NPS benefit that works under the new tax regime; (c) the annuity threshold asymmetry: exit at 60 needs 40% annuitised; exit before 60 needs 80%; (d) partial withdrawal is from own contributions only, not from total corpus.

3 · Rules Common to All Post Office Schemes

These cut across schemes and are frequently tested because candidates study schemes in isolation.

Governing framework. The Government Savings Banks Act, 1873 was renamed the Government Savings Promotion Act in 2018, consolidating the PPF Act 1968 and the Government Savings Certificates Act 1959. Each scheme's operative rules were re-notified in 2019. Rates are set quarterly by the Department of Economic Affairs, benchmarked to G-Sec yields of comparable maturity plus a spread (the Shyamala Gopinath committee formula).
Rule areaPosition

Silent / dormant accounts SB: no transaction for 3 consecutive FYs. RD: discontinued after 4 consecutive defaults. PPF/SSY: irregular (default) if annual minimum not deposited; revive with ₹50 penalty per defaulted year + arrears.
Court attachment protection PPF balance uniquely immune from court attachment under any decree for the holder's debts. This protection does not extend to any other scheme.

4 · Tax Treatment at a Glance

6 · Memory Hooks

Which schemes pay regular cash? Only two: MIS (monthly) and SCSS (quarterly). TD pays annually. All others are cumulative — nothing until maturity.
Which have no joint account option? PPF · SSY · MSSC · NPS. SCSS is the half-case: spouse only. Everything else allows up to 3 adults.
Which have no extension at all? MIS, NSC, KVP, SSY. Extendable: RD (+5 yrs), TD (same tenure), SCSS (+3 yrs, unlimited blocks), PPF (+5 yrs, unlimited blocks).
The three EEE instruments: PPF · SSY · NPS lump sum (up to 60%). Nothing else in the small savings universe is fully exempt on all three events.
Penalty style — two groups: MIS and SCSS deduct from principal (2%/1% and 1.5%/1% respectively). TD and RD cut the interest rate (to TD rate–2% and SB rate respectively). PPF reduces the rate by 1%. MSSC drops to 5.5% flat.
Who allows NRIs? Only NPS. Every post office small savings scheme bars NRIs from opening a new account.
Court attachment immunity: Only PPF has this. Not SCSS, not SSY, not NPS — only PPF.
NSC vs KVP — the tax flip: NSC → 80C deduction + deemed reinvestment benefit. KVP → zero tax benefit, but liquidity after 30 months and capital doubling. Same rate (7.5% for KVP vs 7.7% for NSC), vastly different tax profiles.
