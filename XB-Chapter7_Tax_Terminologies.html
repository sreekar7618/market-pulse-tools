NRI, NR & Indian Tax — The Complete Picture

Everything from your Chapter 7 sessions — key concepts, residential status, scope of income, FEMA vs Income Tax, deemed resident, and the CRS trap. Corrected based on official Income Tax material and PwC guidance.

🔑 Key Concepts
PY vs AY · Person · Assessee · Income · Five Heads of Income
Sec 3 · Previous Year (PY)
The year income is earned

FY 2024-25 = Apr 1 2024 → Mar 31 2025. Tax is calculated for this year.

Sec 2(9) · Assessment Year (AY)
PY + 1 — the year of filing

If PY = 2024-25, then AY = 2025-26. Tax is filed and assessed in the AY.

Memory hook: PY = the year you earned. AY = the year the tax man assesses. Always PY + 1 = AY.
Sec 2(31) — 7 Types of Person
IndividualHUFCompany Partnership / LLPAOP / BOI Local AuthorityArtificial Juridical Person
"I Have Cash, Pretty Ladies Also Admire"
Individual · HUF · Company · Partnership · Local authority · AOP/BOI · Artificial juridical person
Sec 2(7) — Assessee

Anyone liable to pay tax or any sum under the Act, OR any person in respect of whom any proceeding has been initiated — for income, loss, or refund.

Sec 2(24) — Income (inclusive definition)

Listed items plus anything in the nature of income. Includes PGBP, dividends, capital gains, gifts from non-relatives (Sec 56), perquisites from employment.

Five Heads of Income — expand each
1 Income from Salary — Sec 17 +
Requires employer–employee relationship. Without it → income assessed under PGBP or IFOS.

Includes: wages, pension, gratuity, perquisites, employer PF contribution >12%, NPS contribution, leave encashment.

Key deductions: Employer NPS up to 14% of salary → Sec 80CCD(2). Government Agniveer corpus → Sec 80CCH (available even under new regime).
2 Income from House Property — Sec 22-27 +
Rental income from buildings or land appurtenant thereto. Annual value is the computation base. Self-occupied property has deemed income rules. Section 24(b) allows interest deduction on home loan.
3 PGBP — Profits & Gains from Business or Profession +
Covers business income, professional income, F&O trading. F&O is classified as non-speculative PGBP. If income doesn't fit Salary or House Property, check here before IFOS.
4 Capital Gains — Sec 45–55A +
Profit on transfer of a capital asset. STCG vs LTCG depends on holding period. Tax treatment varies by security type. Equity LTCG taxed at 12.5% above ₹1.25L (post Budget 2024). Capital loss can only set off against capital gain.
5 Income from Other Sources (IFOS) — Sec 56 +
Residual head — anything not covered under the four heads above. Includes: gifts from non-relatives >₹50,000, property received below market price, dividend, FD interest, lottery winnings.
⚠ Exam trap: Citizenship ≠ Residential status. A UK citizen can be a tax resident of India. Always evaluate Income Tax residency, FEMA status, and immigration law as three completely independent determinations.

HUF Residential Status — Simpler Rule
Resident

Control and management wholly or partly in India. Even one decision taken in India = Resident. RNOR further depends on Karta's status.

Non-Resident

Control and management wholly outside India. Even one India decision breaks this — no partial rule for HUF.

RNOR — The Returning NRI Buffer (why it exists)

The law recognises that someone returning after years abroad shouldn't be immediately hit with worldwide taxation the moment they cross 182 days. RNOR is a transition buffer — you're physically resident but your foreign income stays protected while you re-establish roots.

Dubai example — 10 years abroad, then returning: Years 1–10: In Dubai → NR every year (RNOR irrelevant — not even a Resident) Year 11: Return, 200d → Resident → Gate 2 → NR for 9/10 years → RNOR Year 12: Stay 300d → Resident → Gate 2 → RNOR conditions still met → RNOR Year 13-14: Gate 2 conditions start failing → becomes ROR
Key insight: RNOR is NOT about being abroad this year. It tests how deeply rooted you are in India based on your past years — once you have already crossed into Resident territory.

One-liner: NRI = FEMA's word (where you live & work). NR = Income Tax's word (how many days in India). Two laws, two separate answers for the same person.
Classic divergence — Dubai job, visited India 200 days
Facts: Working in Dubai (UAE employment visa) but visited India 200 days this FY. Same person. Same year. NRI under FEMA. Resident under Income Tax. This is the most tested distinction in exams and in real professional practice.

You are an Indian citizen
2

Shell company — three-layer government attack
1st
POEM — Place of Effective Management
If a foreign company's real control and management is in India → treated as an Indian company → income taxable in India. Raj sitting in India making all decisions → Dubai company's POEM = India → ₹5 crore taxable as Indian company income.
2nd
GAAR — General Anti Avoidance Rule
If the main purpose of an arrangement is to get a tax benefit with no real commercial substance → tax authorities ignore the entity entirely → tax the income as if the Dubai company never existed → ₹5 crore taxed directly in Raj's hands.
3rd
Sec 6(1A) — Deemed Resident
Even if POEM and GAAR somehow fail → Indian citizen + income > ₹15L + zero tax anywhere → Deemed Resident → RNOR → Indian income taxable. The last line of defence.

💼 The Work-From-Anywhere Tax Trap
UAE visa + 300 days in India · DTAA zero relief · What to actually do
The scenario — thousands of post-COVID professionals are in this trap
Facts: ✓ Indian citizen ✓ Valid UAE employment visa + UAE residence visa ✓ Income credited to UAE bank → zero tax in UAE ✓ Physically present in India → ~300 days per FY ✓ Visits UAE every 6 months just to maintain residency stamp ✓ Nominal income in India
Income Tax Analysis — no escape via visa or bank location
Gate 1: Days in India = 300 days → 300 > 182 → RESIDENT Gate 2 — ROR or RNOR? NR for 9 of last 10 years? → Probably NO (recently started arrangement) ≤ 729 days in last 7 years? → 300d × 7 = 2100 >> 729 → FAILS Result: ROR — Worldwide income taxable in India UAE salary sitting in Dubai bank = FULLY TAXABLE IN INDIA UAE visa = irrelevant to Indian Income Tax Act Zero UAE tax = zero DTAA relief
Hard truth: UAE visa, Dubai bank account, UAE work contract — none of it matters to Indian Income Tax. 300 days = Resident = ROR = worldwide income taxable. Full stop.
Why DTAA gives zero relief here
How DTAA normally works: Both countries want to tax the same income → You get credit for tax paid in Country A, offset against Country B's tax The problem here: UAE taxes income at 0% India taxes at slab rate (say 30%) DTAA credit = credit for tax paid in UAE = ₹0 → Full Indian slab tax applies with zero offset DTAA prevents double taxation — not single taxation by India alone If one country charges zero, there is nothing to offset
PerspectiveStatusConsequence
Income TaxRORUAE salary fully taxable in India at slab rate
FEMALikely NRICan hold NRE account, repatriate freely
UAEResident (visa)Zero tax there
RealityPays tax nowhereHuge risk — CRS will flag it (see next section)
What he should actually do — three options
1
Reduce India stay to < 182 days — becomes NR → UAE income not taxable in India. This is the only clean, legally safe solution.
2
Accept ROR status — declare UAE income in India, pay tax at slab rates, maintain proper records and documentation.
3
Do nothing — high risk. CRS will eventually flag it. Back taxes + 12% interest per annum + up to 300% penalty. Criminal prosecution in serious cases.
🔍 CRS — How You Get Caught Without PAN
UAE banks never ask for PAN or Aadhaar — but passport number is enough. Here's the exact mechanism.
What UAE banks collect and report under CRS (no PAN needed)
Full legal name Date of birth Country of birth Nationality Passport number ← the bridge Residential address (as declared) Account balance Income credited to account
How CBDT links passport number to your PAN
CRS data arrives from UAE: Name + Date of Birth + Passport Number + Account balance CBDT matching algorithm: Passport number → cross-referenced against: ├── Passport database (Ministry of External Affairs) ├── PAN database (Income Tax Department) └── Aadhaar database (UIDAI) → PAN identified → Your ITR pulled up automatically → Mismatch flagged for action
Passport number is the bridge. Every time you applied for a passport, filed ITR with travel details, or bought forex at an Indian bank — the PAN-passport linkage was established in government databases.
4 additional detection routes beyond CRS
1
Forex purchases in India — Every transaction >$10,000 reported under FEMA with PAN captured at point of purchase. Creates a foreign currency movement trail.
2
Schedule FA & FSI in ITR — Specifically asks: "Foreign bank account? Foreign assets? Foreign income?" Lying here = deliberate concealment = criminal offence under Income Tax Act.
3
Air travel data — Every international departure/arrival logged by Bureau of Immigration, linked to passport. CBDT can independently verify exact days outside India and cross-check your residential status claim.
4
SWIFT transfers — If Dubai salary comes to India via bank transfer → SWIFT → correspondent bank → Indian bank → reported under FEMA above threshold. Source of funds trail established.
How a notice gets generated — exact 6-step sequence
1
UAE bank reports your account to UAE Ministry of Finance annually under CRS obligations.
2
UAE MoF sends data to India CBDT under CRS / FATCA framework — automatically, every year.
3
CBDT algorithm matches Name + DOB + Passport → finds your PAN.
4
System checks your ITR — Foreign income declared? NO. Foreign bank account declared? NO. → Mismatch flagged automatically.
5
Automated notice under Section 148A issued — "We have information suggesting undisclosed income." Must respond within 30 days.
6
Unsatisfactory response → Assessment opened → Tax + 12% interest per annum + penalty 100% to 300% of tax evaded + possible prosecution.

6 most common exam traps — corrected edition
1
Citizenship ≠ Residency. Foreign citizen can be tax resident of India. Always evaluate Income Tax, FEMA, and immigration law separately — three independent determinations.
2
Clause (d) — 120 days — directly produces RNOR, not ROR. The statute locks the outcome to RNOR when residency is triggered solely by the 120-day rule. It never produces ROR. (Corrected from earlier version.)
3
Deemed resident = always RNOR. Never ROR. Even if he satisfies zero RNOR conditions normally. Hardcoded by Sec 6(1A).
4
HUF: even one decision in India = Resident. Only "wholly outside" = NR. There is no partial or proportional rule for HUF.
5
DTAA gives zero relief if the other country's tax rate is zero. DTAA prevents double taxation — not single taxation by India alone. UAE's 0% rate = ₹0 credit.
6
Indian income is always taxable for all three statuses. ROR, RNOR, and NR all pay tax on Indian-sourced income. Only treatment of foreign income differs across statuses.
